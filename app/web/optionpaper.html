Option paper trading

The scanner's signals traded with options, in their own paper account. A BUY signal buys a call (CE), a SELL signal buys a put (PE); options are never sold here. The order waits for the stock to reach the entry; the stop, the target and the trailing stop follow the stock's price, and the option is sold at its live price. On the expiry day the position is closed at the time set below. Paper only: no order is ever sent to your broker.

Option paper settings

Trailing uses the Lock or Step settings of the Settings page, on the stock's price, at once on each live price. Charges use the brokerage, SEBI, GST and stamp duty of the Settings page, with STT of 0.1% on the sell premium.
